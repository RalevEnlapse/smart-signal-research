## Document Structure

Each research document follows a standardized structure:

1. **Executive summary** — High-level overview and key takeaways
2. **Background and context** — Historical context and problem statement
3. **Stakeholders** — Key stakeholders and their roles
4. **Threat model / abuse cases** — Security and operational threats with mitigations
5. **Reference architecture** — System components and data flows
6. **Methods / algorithms / standards** — Technical approaches and industry standards
7. **Data requirements** — Data types, quality requirements, and sources
8. **Implementation plan** — Phased approach with timelines
9. **Testing and validation** — Validation strategies and quality assurance
10. **Observability (SLIs/SLOs)** — Monitoring metrics and service level objectives
11. **Governance and compliance** — Decision rights and regulatory requirements
12. **Risks and mitigations** — Risk assessment and mitigation strategies
13. **Costs and FinOps** — Implementation and operating costs with ROI analysis
14. **KPIs** — Key performance indicators for success measurement
15. **Deliverables and checklists** — Concrete deliverables and implementation checklists
16. **References** — Source documents, external references, and suggested reading
