# Smart culture & heritage — Deep research

## Executive summary
Smart culture and heritage initiatives digitally preserve cultural assets, create virtual museums, and enhance cultural experiences through immersive technologies. These platforms enable the preservation of cultural heritage, increased accessibility to cultural resources, innovative visitor experiences, and new forms of cultural engagement that bridge physical and digital realms.

The critical technical outputs are: (1) digital preservation systems, (2) virtual and augmented reality experiences, (3) cultural asset management platforms, and (4) interactive visitor engagement tools. Achieving these requires digitization technologies, immersive media platforms, content management systems, and integration with cultural institutions.

This document deepens item 39 in [`kali-task-research.md`](../kali-task-research.md:1): *"Smart culture & heritage: Digitally preserve cultural assets, create virtual museums, and enhance cultural experiences through immersive technologies."*

## 1. Background and context
Cultural heritage preservation faces significant challenges:
- Physical degradation of artifacts and sites
- Limited accessibility to cultural resources
- Declining visitor engagement
- High costs of preservation
- Loss of intangible cultural heritage

A smart culture and heritage approach supports:
- Digital preservation of cultural assets
- Enhanced accessibility and inclusion
- Innovative visitor experiences
- New forms of cultural engagement
- Sustainable preservation practices
